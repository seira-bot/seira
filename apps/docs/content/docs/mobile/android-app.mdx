The Seira app for Android is called "Seira: AI bot companion". It is a companion for Seira, not a second copy of it.

## What the app does

On your phone you can:

- Read your bots and their conversations.
- Answer approvals and questions from your bots.
- Send a new task to a bot.

Your bots, credentials, and transcripts stay on your computer or on Seira Cloud. The phone keeps no copy of them.

## What you need

- The Android app from Google Play.
- Seira on a computer, or Seira Cloud.

The computer must be on, awake, and running Seira. Seira Cloud works while your computer is off.

## Pair the phone with your computer

1. On your computer, open Seira and go to **Settings → Phone & devices**.
2. Look at the QR code that Seira shows.
3. On your phone, open the Seira app and choose **Scan QR Code**.
4. Check the computer name on the phone, then confirm.

If the camera cannot scan the code, enter the address and the code that **Phone & devices** shows. Use the same Wi-Fi network for both devices, or use Tailscale on both devices when you are away.

Each pairing is separate. You can pair the phone with more than one computer and switch between them.

## Pair the phone with Seira Cloud

1. In the Seira desktop app, open the menu under your name.
2. Choose **Connect your phone · to the Cloud (always on)**.
3. Choose **Create pairing code**.
4. Scan the QR code with the Seira app on your phone.

## Remove a phone

Open **Settings → Phone & devices** on your computer and remove the phone. It cannot reach your bots after that. The phone can also unpair itself in the app settings.
